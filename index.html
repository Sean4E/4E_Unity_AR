AR journey guide · Unity 6 · AR Foundation

From an empty project to content locked onto the real world.

A guided route through Unity 6 and AR Foundation for Android and iOS. Tell it your device, follow the landmarks, and a one-click builder sets up a sample with coaching, tap-to-place, anchors, touch gestures, a long-press menu, colour variants, image tracking, light estimation, occlusion and physics on real surfaces.
